## Research Scope Definition

This research aims to provide a comprehensive understanding of hyperlocal community platforms. The scope includes:

*   **Features:** Identifying common and innovative features that enhance community engagement and support local businesses.
*   **Target Audience:** Defining the demographics and psychographics of typical users of hyperlocal platforms.
*   **Monetization Strategies:** Analyzing various methods for generating revenue, including advertising, subscriptions, and partnerships.
*   **Best Practices:** Determining the key strategies and tactics for building and managing a successful hyperlocal platform.
*   **Community Engagement:** Investigating methods to foster a strong sense of community and encourage active participation.
*   **Local Business Support:** Exploring ways the platform can effectively support and promote local businesses.
