# Secondary Findings

This document summarizes secondary findings from the initial data collection phase, focusing on community platform recommendations and features.

## Community Platform Recommendations (StoryPrompt)

StoryPrompt recommends the following platforms:

1.  **Swarm:** Best for Coaches, Creators, & Solopreneurs (video-centric).
2.  **Circle:** Best for B2B Businesses & Entrepreneurs.
3.  **Disco:** Best for AI-Based Social Learning.
4.  **Mighty Networks:** Best for Course Creators.
5.  **Skool:** Best for Gamification.
6.  **Bettermode:** Highly customizable and easy to follow.
7.  **Facebook Groups:** Free to use but limited control over data.
8.  **Slack:** Text-based communication tool, good for quick questions.
9.  **Thinkific:** Great for linking online courses and community.
10. **Zapnito:** Learning community, good for hosting content.
11. **Reign Theme:** WordPress theme for creating community websites.
12. **Disciple:** Clean and easy to navigate.
13. **Insided:** Integrates customer community content with a knowledge base.
14. **Hivebrite:** Specific community use-cases.
15. **Uscreen:** Primarily a platform for creators to sell video.
16. **Guild:** Simple to use and delivers exactly what it promises.
17. **MeltingSpot:** Good for hosting live events.
18. **Podia:** Simple, but uninspiring.

## Community Platform Recommendations (LinoDash)

LinoDash recommends the following platforms:

1.  **Circle.so:** Best all-in-one community platform.
2.  **Mighty Networks:** Best for building multi-level communities.
3.  **Kajabi:** Best for knowledge entrepreneurs and course creators.
4.  **GroupApp:** Best for building learning communities.
5.  **Swarm.to:** Best for video-centric online communities.
6.  **Skool:** Best for gamification and community discoverability.
7.  **Podia:** Best for selling digital products.
8.  **Bettermode:** Best for customer-centric communities.
9.  **Slack:** Best free community software for starters.
