# Research Scope Definition

This research aims to provide a comprehensive understanding of the requirements, users, features, information architecture, look and feel, platform, rules, success criteria, inspirations, and future dreams for a hyperlocal community events site and marketplace, as outlined in the blueprint document.

The research will focus on:

*   Understanding the needs and goals of the primary users (community members, local businesses, organizations, and platform administrators).
*   Identifying the core features and functionalities of the platform.
*   Defining the information architecture and data relationships.
*   Determining the desired look and feel of the platform.
*   Understanding the technical requirements and constraints.
*   Identifying potential inspirations and comparisons from existing platforms.
*   Defining success criteria for the platform.
