# Key Research Questions

This document outlines the key questions that this research aims to answer in order to gain a comprehensive understanding of the requirements, users, features, and technical considerations for the hyperlocal community events site and marketplace.

**General Questions:**

*   What are the most important features for a successful hyperlocal community platform?
*   What are the key challenges in building and maintaining such a platform?
*   What are the best practices for user engagement and community building in a hyperlocal context?
*   How can the platform effectively connect residents, businesses, and organizations?
*   What are the most effective monetization strategies for a platform of this type?

**User-Related Questions:**

*   What are the specific needs and pain points of each user group (community members, businesses, organizations, administrators)?
*   How can the platform be designed to be user-friendly and accessible to all user groups?
*   What are the best ways to encourage user participation and content creation?
*   How can the platform ensure user safety and privacy?

**Technical Questions:**

*   What are the most suitable technologies and platforms for building this application?
*   How can the platform be designed to be scalable and maintainable?
*   What are the key security considerations for the platform?
*   How can the platform be optimized for performance and speed?
