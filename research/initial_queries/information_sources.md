# Potential Information Sources

This document brainstorms potential information sources for researching the requirements, users, features, and technical considerations for the hyperlocal community events site and marketplace.

**General Information Sources:**

*   **Online search engines:** Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo
*   **Academic databases:** Google Scholar, JSTOR, ScienceDirect
*   **Industry publications:** Articles, blog posts, and reports on community platforms, online marketplaces, and local businesses.
*   **Community forums and social media groups:** Reddit, Facebook groups, Nextdoor

**Specific Information Sources:**

*   **Existing community platforms:** Nextdoor, Meetup, Facebook Groups/Events, Eventbrite, local Craigslist/Kijiji
*   **Documentation for relevant technologies:** Supabase, JavaScript frameworks (React, Angular, Vue.js), mapping APIs (Google Maps, Leaflet)
*   **Case studies of successful hyperlocal platforms:** Research and analyze successful examples of similar platforms.
*   **Expert interviews:** Interview experts in community building, online marketplaces, and hyperlocal businesses.

**Targeted Search Terms:**

*   Hyperlocal community platform
*   Online marketplace best practices
*   Community engagement strategies
*   Local business marketing
*   Event management software
*   Supabase tutorial
*   React tutorial
*   User interface design principles
