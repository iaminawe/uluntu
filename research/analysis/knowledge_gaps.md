# Knowledge Gaps

This document outlines the knowledge gaps identified during the initial data collection and analysis phase.

*   **Specific Technology Stack:** What specific technologies and frameworks are best suited for building the hyperlocal community events site and marketplace, considering scalability, security, and maintainability?
*   **Monetization Strategies:** What are the most effective monetization strategies for a hyperlocal community platform, and how can these be implemented without alienating users?
*   **Moderation and Content Management:** What are the best practices for moderating content and managing user behavior in a hyperlocal community, and how can these be implemented effectively?
*   **Integration with Local Businesses:** How can the platform effectively integrate with local businesses to provide value and encourage participation?
*   **Long-Term Sustainability:** What are the key factors that contribute to the long-term sustainability of a hyperlocal community platform, and how can these be addressed proactively?
*   **Specific Needs of Each User Group:** What are the specific needs and pain points of each user group (community members, businesses, organizations, administrators) in the context of *this* specific hyperlocal community?
*   **Security Best Practices for other Tech Stacks:** What are the security best practices, vulnerabilities, and mitigation strategies for other technology stacks besides MERN stack, such as .NET, Python-Django, and serverless architectures?
